Our story

Built on trust, not turnover

Superb Painting started in 2000 with a straightforward idea: Melbourne homeowners deserve painters who do what they say, respect your home, and deliver a finish worth showing off.

We're still a tight-knit team — not a call centre, not a franchise. Whether it's one bedroom or a full interior and exterior, you get the same crew, the same standards, and the same care.

That consistency is why clients refer us to neighbours, book us back for the next room, and leave five-star reviews across Melbourne.
